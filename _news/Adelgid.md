Our paper, "Chromosome scale genomes of two invasive Adelges species enable virtual screening for selective adelgicides" has been accepted in G3. This work describes the genomes of two adelgids, _Adelges abietis_ and _Adelges tsugae_, and potential targets for selective adelgicides.
